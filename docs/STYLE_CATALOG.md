## Web discovery / 全网扩展 · 2026-09-12

Original real-time studies inspired by the following documented mechanisms. Palette and particle geometry are our interpretation, not claims of exact reconstruction. No footage or audio recordings are copied. 网页描述用于设计原创算法，未训练模型或复制原作音轨。

| Preset / 预设 | Documented reference / 来源 | Our implementation / 实现 |
|---|---|---|
| 数据条码 / Data Stripes | [Ryoji Ikeda: test pattern](https://www.ryojiikeda.com/project/testpattern/) converts sound/data into binary and barcode patterns | Grayscale particle columns, sound-driven width/drift; smooth motion |
| 谐振光场 / Harmonic Light | [Memo Akten: Simple Harmonic Motion](https://memo.tv/projects/2019/shm/) combines simple oscillators into complex audiovisual behavior | Lissajous filaments, drifting phase, resonant synthesis |
| 流动乐谱 / Flowing Score | [Quayola et al.: Partitura–Ligeti](https://quayola.com/partitura-ligeti/) is custom-software real-time sound visualization | Parallel particle ribbons, pitch curvature, onset displacement |
| 机械微动 / Kinetic Array | [Zimoun official biography](https://www.zimoun.net/cv/) describes mechanical rhythm, industrial materials and ordered/chaotic motion | Pendulum-like arrays, asynchronous jitter, noise grains |

Additional discovery: [Tsinghua Art Museum artist lecture](https://www.artmuseum.tsinghua.edu.cn/ggjy/ztjz/jzyy/201703/t20170309_2007.shtml), [Ryoichi Kurokawa unfold.alt](https://ryoichikurokawa.com/project/ualt.html). Candidates, not additional implemented presets.
